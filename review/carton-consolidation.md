# Carton category consolidation

Removed the three food-specific cardboard-box listings and Printed Corrugated Boxes (pizza photography and food application). Products were not moved to another collection. Old URLs redirect to the carton category, and old quote IDs select that category.

Consolidated the remaining eight listings into three families: Mono carton boxes, Pharmaceutical cartons, and Corrugated mailer boxes. Flattened existing aliases to the new canonical product URLs. Preserved original records in carton-source-records.json.

No distinct SKU is established by these family groupings. The mailer family is based on source photography; imported paperboard specifications conflict with that format. Its availability, corrugated construction, ply, strength and size remain explicitly unconfirmed. Pharmacy records also conflict and no conflicting shape or application is promoted as verified. Material descriptions now distinguish board type from board weight. Source galleries retained for review; no new images generated in this structural update.
