# Large cardboard packaging family

Added Cardboard packaging boxes under Cartons & corrugated boxes, combining the earlier large plain corrugated shipping cartons (product-131.webp) and printed deep corrugated carton (product-4.webp / product-53.webp). These photographs were previously mixed into generic mono-carton galleries. Source records remain in carton-source-records.json.

The old brown kraft mono carton source URL and its historical duplicate now redirect to this family because their primary image showed the large printed corrugated box. The mixed mono-carton-box URL remains mapped to folding mono cartons; its shipping-carton photograph contributes to the new family without changing that alias. Existing mono carton, pharma and mailer families remain distinct. Small food-specific boxes remain removed.

No dimensions, ply, strength, capacity or MOQ are inferred from the old inconsistent records. Two new illustrative WebP renders and the category's existing reduced-motion-aware 3D hover support present plain and printed formats.
